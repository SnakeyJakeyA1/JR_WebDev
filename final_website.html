Goblin Valley

Info

Located in Emery County Utah, Goblin Valley, a very popular and commonly visited National Park, is a valley of unnaturally formed sandstone in the shape of goblins. It is a beautiful desert that goes on for miles. At Goblin Valley, many activities can be participated in. Here is a list of fun activities that might get you interested.

Activities:

  • Camping
  • Hiking
  • Biking
  • Backpacking
  • Canyoneering
  • Disc-Golf
  • OHV
  • Stargazing
  • Vacation times
  • Temperatures
