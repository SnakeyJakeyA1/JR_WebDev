Parkour

Parkour is a sport that you don't often hear of a lot. A basic discription of Parkour would be getting from point A to point B as complex and stylish as possible using stunts and tricks to guide you there. There are many techniques with many different stunts that can allow you to get to point B as falshiest as possible. Here is a quick list of each of these techniques, and some examples.

Techniques

Flips

We all know what flips are. Frontflips, backflips, sideflips, fulls, flyaways, all of these are different flips. They each can add not only flare to your stunts, but also can be good flow.

Flow

Flow is an extremeley helpful element in parkour. Flow allows you to be able to go from one trick to another without any staggers or stutters. In other words, as soon as you're done with one stunt, you're immediately setup for another right after.

Safety

A lot of people that I meet say that they're too scared to do parkour because they don't want to end up breaking their necks. It might surprise you to say that parkour is actually a lot safer than you think.

Falling Safely

Firstmost in learning parkour is to fall safely. What this means is taking procedures and practices to help you know how to bail or fail a trick without getting injured.

Drills

Drills are essential for both learning the skill, and being safe and comfortable while doing the stunt. How to do a drill is to practice each part of a stunt seperately and learn how to put them all together.

Summary

Parkour isn't just running around, flipping and vaulting to your hearts content. There's a lot of practice into it and key elements that help you be safer and flashier.
